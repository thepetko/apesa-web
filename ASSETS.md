## Prompt úvodnej fotografie

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset: temporary editorial hero photograph for Slovak nonprofit APESA offering practical training to teachers and psychologists. Wide landscape 3:2 composition, candid documentary feel in a real Central European school classroom. On the RIGHT TWO THIRDS of the image, a female facilitator about 45 with short brown hair and a natural blue shirt demonstrates a small hands-on sorting activity to three adult educators aged 35-60 around a light wooden classroom table. They are engaged with each other and actual learning materials, not looking at camera. Colored paper cards, wooden blocks, notebooks on table, modest school shelving, children's artwork pinned in background, natural daylight. Warm, grounded, lively but professional. LEFT THIRD is a quiet shadowed blue classroom wall with softly blurred shelves and spare space for HTML headline overlay, no important faces there. Eye level, medium-wide 35mm reportage photography, believable facial textures, anatomically natural hands. No writing, no typography, no branding, no watermark. Not corporate office, not business meeting, no laptops, no staged smiling in a row, no plastic stock-photo aesthetic. The photograph must show people actually LEARNING by doing. Full bleed photographic image only, no web UI. High quality.
